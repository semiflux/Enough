Enough is a per-app screen time limiter. Give an app a daily quota, blocked
hours, or both, and Enough steps in when the limit is reached. Apps you did
not add are never touched.

<b>Limits</b>
Daily quota, for example 25 minutes of Instagram a day, counted from
midnight in your own timezone. A notification reminds you five minutes
before the quota runs out.

Blocked hours, for example no YouTube from 07:00 to 12:00 on weekdays.
Windows are set per weekday. When a quota and a window overlap, the window
wins.

While an app is blocked you get a full screen notice with a Close button,
and a notification.

<b>How it counts</b>
Usage is integrated from raw UsageEvents, clipped to local midnight, with
per-activity resume and pause events paired up. It does not use Android's
daily usage buckets, which are rolling 24 hour windows, so the number does
not jump when the system resets them.

<b>How blocking is enforced</b>
A foreground service watches the foreground app while the screen is on. If
you also enable the accessibility service, blocking is driven by
accessibility events and happens immediately instead of on the next check.
Android exposes no way for a normal app to observe app usage, so the check
is a poll.

<b>What it does not do</b>
No usage history or statistics, no website blocking, no accounts, no
telemetry. The app declares no INTERNET permission.
